Community Riggers

<

Community Riggers is a directory of IATSE stage riggers working across the Bay Area and Greater Northern California. The aim is the help keep track of riggers who work outside their home local, often across multiple venues between San Jose, San Francisco, Oakland, and Sacramento. Because they move between locals, it can be hard to track who's actually available. This site is a centralized, opt-in resource to help business agents and call stewards find qualified riggers quickly.

Participation is 100% voluntary. You must add yourself. Once added, you'll get a personal link (which you can bookmark) to update your info and availability any time. The system is only as useful as it is up to date, so keep it current.

This is an independent project, not affiliated with any IATSE local, and not a substitute for keeping your availability updated with your home local. It's meant to help fill last-minute openings during busy periods.

Questions? Email admin@communityriggers.org.
