Terms of Service

Community Riggers is an independent, volunteer-run directory of IATSE stage riggers working across the Bay Area and Greater Northern California. It is not affiliated with any IATSE local.

Participation

Adding yourself to the directory is entirely voluntary. You may request removal at any time by emailing admin@communityriggers.org.

SMS Messaging

By providing your phone number and opting in, you agree to receive SMS text messages from Community Riggers related to your availability, including your personal availability link and occasional reminders. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out at any time. Consent to receive messages is not a condition of any purchase, and there is nothing to purchase — this service is free.

No Warranty

This service is provided as-is, without warranty of any kind. It is meant to help fill last-minute openings and is not a substitute for keeping your availability updated with your home local.

Contact

Questions? Email admin@communityriggers.org.
