Privacy Policy

What We Collect

When you add yourself to the Community Riggers directory, we collect your name, phone number, IATSE local affiliation, city, and the availability you set for the next several days.

How We Use It

Your information is used only to operate this directory: showing business agents and call stewards who is available to rig, and sending you SMS messages related to your availability (your personal link and occasional reminders) via our messaging provider, Twilio. We do not sell or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

SMS Opt-Out

You can stop receiving text messages at any time by replying STOP to any message. Reply HELP for assistance.

Removing Your Information

You can request removal from the directory at any time by emailing admin@communityriggers.org.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email admin@communityriggers.org.
